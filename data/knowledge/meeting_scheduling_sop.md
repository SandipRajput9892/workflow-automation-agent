# SOP: Scheduling Meetings

How to book meetings with customers and colleagues. Owner: Operations.

## Times

- Book meetings on weekdays between 09:00 and 17:00 local time. Never book on weekends or public holidays.
- Customer meetings start on the hour or half hour. The default length is 30 minutes; demos are 45 minutes.
- If a task asks for a time outside business hours, book the nearest slot inside them and say so.

## Titles

Use the format `<Meeting type> - <Company>`, for example "Product demo - Globex" or "Kickoff call - Nimbus Labs". Internal meetings use a short, descriptive title such as "Q4 pipeline kickoff".

## Attendees

- Always invite the external contact and the OurCo account owner (Jordan, jordan@ourco.com, for sales meetings).
- Do not invite more than 5 people to a customer meeting without a manager's approval.

## After booking

- Email external attendees a confirmation with the title, date, time and time zone. Follow the Customer Email Guidelines.
- Post customer meetings in **#sales**. Post internal team meetings in **#general**.
