# Lead Management Policy

Applies to everyone who adds or updates leads in the CRM. Owner: Sales Operations.

## One lead per person

- Every person has exactly one lead, identified by their email address. Never create a second lead for someone who is already in the CRM.
- If the person already exists, reuse the existing lead ID (for example LEAD-0001) and update that record instead.
- Record the person's full name and the company's official name (e.g. "Acme Corp", not "acme").

## Pipeline statuses

Use these statuses, in this order:

- **new**: added to the CRM, no conversation yet.
- **contacted**: we have spoken or met with them, or sent a personal follow-up. Set this after any first meeting or call.
- **qualified**: they have a confirmed need, budget and timeline.
- **proposal**: a written proposal or quote has been sent.
- **won** / **lost**: the deal is closed.

Do not skip straight from new to proposal, won or lost without a note from the account owner.

## Announcing leads

- Every new lead, and every lead that moves to qualified or later, is announced in **#sales**.
- The announcement includes the lead ID, the person's name and title, the company and the current status.
- Do not post a lead's email address or phone number in Slack. The CRM record is the place for contact details.
