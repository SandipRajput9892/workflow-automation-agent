# Customer Email Guidelines

How OurCo writes to customers, leads and partners. Owner: Marketing.

## Tone

- Warm, professional and concise. Aim for under 150 words.
- Address the person by first name ("Hi Priya,").
- Write in plain language. No jargon, no exclamation marks in subject lines, no emojis.

## Subject lines

- Say what the email is about in under 60 characters.
- Follow-ups after a meeting: "Great meeting you, <First name>" or "Following up on our conversation".
- Meeting confirmations: "Confirmed: <Meeting title> on <date>".

## Structure

1. A personal opening that refers to the meeting or conversation.
2. One or two sentences of substance: what we discussed, or what we will do.
3. Exactly one clear next step, such as a suggested call, a demo or a document we will send.
4. The standard sign-off.

## Sign-off

Every external email ends with:

Best regards,
The OurCo Team

## What not to include

- Never promise prices, discounts, delivery dates or contract terms. Say the account owner will follow up on commercial details.
- Never attach or promise documents we have not been asked to send.
- Never copy other customers on an email, and never reveal other customers' names.
