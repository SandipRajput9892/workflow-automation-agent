# SOP: Sales Follow-up After a Meeting

Standard operating procedure for the first follow-up after meeting a prospect. Owner: Head of Sales.

## When it applies

After any first meeting, call or event conversation with a potential customer.

## Steps

1. **CRM**: make sure the person is a lead in the CRM (see the Lead Management Policy; never create duplicates). Set the status to **contacted**.
2. **Follow-up email**: within 24 hours, email the person to thank them for their time, recap the one or two topics that mattered most to them, and propose one concrete next step, such as a 30-minute demo next week. Follow the Customer Email Guidelines.
3. **Team update**: post in **#sales** in this format:
   `New lead: <Name> (<Title>, <Company>) - <Lead ID> - status: contacted. Follow-up email sent.`
4. **Next meeting**: if the prospect already agreed to a next meeting, book it in the calendar. Follow the Meeting Scheduling SOP.

## Senior prospects

For VP-level or C-level contacts, also add the account owner, Jordan (jordan@ourco.com), to any meeting invite, and mention Jordan by name in the follow-up email as their point of contact.
