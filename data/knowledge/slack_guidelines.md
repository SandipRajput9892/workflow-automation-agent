# Slack Communication Guidelines

Which channel to use, and what may be posted there. Owner: People Operations.

## Channels

- **#sales**: new leads, pipeline changes, customer meetings and deals.
- **#general**: company-wide announcements and internal meetings.
- **#support**: customer issues and escalations.

Never post customer information in #general.

## Writing posts

- Lead with the news in one line, then add details if needed.
- Refer to CRM records by ID (e.g. LEAD-0001) so teammates can look them up.
- Keep posts short: 1 to 3 lines.

## Privacy

- Do not post customers' email addresses, phone numbers or other personal contact details in any channel.
- Do not post pricing or contract terms outside private deal channels.
